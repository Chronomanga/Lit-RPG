## What “complete” means here

Five main articles, the Reddit opening post, and all 27 returned video caption texts were read end to end. The 27 videos are 26 channel entries plus the separate Sanderson lecture. The Bing result duplicates one channel video. Reddit's displayed discussion was read, but collapsed branches, deleted comments, and every historical reply were not recovered.

Caption-text study is not full audio listening or visual viewing. Automatic captions can miss names and words. Nine vidIQ outputs have no timestamps or track classification; no playback anchors were invented for them. One output ends in a clipped farewell; on-screen lesson text not spoken aloud was not reviewed. The inventory and individual notes identify the actual scope.

No claim is made to have studied linked novels, underlying case-study analytics, external interviews in full, unseen downloadable templates, paid courses, every Sanderson lecture, channel Shorts/live archives, or future uploads. The publicly linked material may change after this research date.

## Evidence and interpretation

- Article and video advice is attributed practitioner opinion unless independently verified. Its presence in several guides does not prove a universal rule.
- The Reddit ten-step post is satire, and comments express individual views.
- Sales, ranking, neuroscience, audience, and platform-mechanism claims were not independently established by these readings.
- Source-specific notes preserve disagreements. [FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md) explains the editorial choices used in our workflow.
- [EXAMPLES.md](EXAMPLES.md), the templates, and the twelve-step process are original implementation aids, not reconstructions of paid or unseen source worksheets.
- Local research inputs and a source-file evidence manifest are preserved outside tracked content. Full source texts, tool logs, account details, and private manuscripts are not part of this public playbook.

Use a source's actual link when referring to its teaching. Recheck current official destination policies before any operational publishing or marketing decision.
