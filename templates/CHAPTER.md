## Bounded writer brief

Supply only relevant approved facts and the current state. Do not attach an unfiltered future outline. Include the style reference, actual prior ending, immediate objective, permitted reveals, and constraints. If no separate drafting context is available, preserve the same boundary and do not call the context blind.

## Draft

Write the complete prose in a separate manuscript file or below a clearly marked manuscript heading. Keep planner notes out of the story text.
