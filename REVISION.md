# Revision and continuity review

Read the actual complete prose before declaring a chapter finished. A checklist organizes judgment; it cannot replace reading.

## Pass 1 — Cause, character, and comprehension

- Can the reader picture the place and follow the important movement?
- What does the viewpoint character want now, and why?
- Which choice changes what happens? Does the result follow?
- Is necessary information established before a decisive use, or credibly discovered during the attempt?
- Does every important new person have a usable identity, role, or present aim?
- Do companions and opponents behave consistently with their own knowledge and motives?
- Does the chapter accomplish a useful result or meaningful change?

Fix structural problems before polishing sentences. If an intended mystery is the only confusing element, verify that it feels intentional and has a basis. Do not explain a future reveal merely to remove all uncertainty.

## Pass 2 — System, physical, and numerical continuity

Trace actions in order. Check valid targets, reach, visibility, duration, cooldowns, costs, recovery, prerequisites, and failure conditions. Check who can see a System message. A private interface is not public evidence without an established sharing mechanism.

Recalculate current and maximum resources separately. Check XP thresholds, overflow, reset behavior, level rewards, allocation, and timing. Track equipment possession, consumed items, injury, fatigue, time, location, and travel. A new maximum is not automatically a refill; a notification is not automatically a heal.

Confirm the physical sequence around the decisive action: approach, object or person reached, attack or ability used, reaction, movement, and result. A character cannot use a weapon still left in another room or know a conversation they did not hear.

## Pass 3 — Flow and explanation

Use common words without flattening emotion. Read dialogue for clear speakers and individual voice. Remove explanations that repeat an already visible result. Keep the rule, history, clue, or emotional detail that makes the next choice understandable.

Listen to the rhythm while reading aloud if possible. Join fragments that merely break one thought into pieces; shorten a tangled sentence when doing so clarifies it. Preserve natural variation. Do not enforce short sentences through a readability score.

Check notification volume against purpose. A complete private ledger does not require a complete status readout after every gain. New power should be felt in action, not only listed.

## Pass 4 — The actual ending and next beginning

Read the last scene. What has happened, what state remains, and what question follows? Check the next chapter's opening against that ending. Track unfinished injuries, debts, threats, loyalties, hidden information, and resources. A planned hook label is not evidence that the prose delivered it.

## Pass 5 — Feedback and final state

Ask a fresh reader focused questions: What does the hero want? What can the power do? What did the choice cost? Who is the new person? What changed? What makes you want to continue? Record the reaction before prescribing a fix.

A simulated reader receives the text and audience brief only. Label the feedback as simulated. If the reviewer already knows future plans, do not call the read blind. Real audience feedback is a different kind of evidence.

After edits, reread the final text, check the affected calculations and dependencies, and then update the authoritative state and progression ledger. Keep a concise record of material changes and open questions. Do not update canon from an abandoned draft.

## Whole-book pass

Check promise fulfillment, protagonist agency, supporting arcs, antagonist action, rule consistency, progression variety, setup/payoff, disclosure order, repeated encounters, inflation, and local closure. An intentional retcon needs a decision and a dependency sweep, not just a new entry in the bible.

Optional tools may catch repeated words, missing numbers, broken links, or ledger errors. Report their scope honestly. A clean script, editorial score, or metadata validator does not prove continuity, originality, natural audio, or reader satisfaction.
