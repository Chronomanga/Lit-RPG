# What the research found

The supplied sources are useful, but they are different kinds of evidence: craft essays, a writing lecture, community satire and discussion, coaching lessons, advertisements, testimonials, and launch case accounts. The notes preserve those distinctions. This synthesis is our editorial judgment, not a claim that all sources agree.

## The most useful lessons

**Character and progression need each other.** A stronger skill matters more when it helps someone pursue a personal goal or forces a difficult choice. Give the protagonist a recognizable way of reacting, useful competence, and initiative. Keep companions and opponents capable of their own decisions. [Article notes](research/ARTICLES-AND-REDDIT.md), [Hatch craft notes](research/HATCH-CRAFT.md).

**The System needs a stable private design.** Decide what stats mean, how classes differ, what earns progress, when leveling takes effect, whether it refills resources, and what abilities can and cannot do. Keep a record, then show the parts that affect the current scene. Later layers can expand the design without contradicting its established core. [System lesson and worksheet](research/HATCH-SYSTEMS-AND-VISIBILITY.md), [web-guide notes](research/WEB-GUIDES.md).

**Growth should change the experience.** Plan useful improvements, discoveries, control, relationships, and judgment alongside numerical gains. Test newly important abilities. A reward can solve one problem while creating a choice or exposing a larger one. Repetitive grinding does not need repeated full scenes. [Earlier articles](research/ARTICLES-AND-REDDIT.md).

**A world grows from consequences.** If powers are real, they affect ordinary work, medicine, transport, danger, authority, and access to resources. Our worldbuilding exercises extend the sources into a practical design method; they are original worksheets rather than a quoted source framework. Explain the consequences the scene needs and stage the larger history.

**Opening functions matter more than fixed placement.** Establish a person, a situation, investment, understandable action, the progression promise, and an initial useful result. An urgent dungeon opening can do this. A particular five-chapter or forty-chapter map is an option to test. [Opening and final-act notes](research/HATCH-CORE-AND-BING.md).

**The middle must keep changing.** New trials, knowledge, relationships, opponent action, responsibility, and consequences can deepen the premise. Keep the protagonist pursuing goals before the midpoint; a later revelation can redirect that pursuit. [Craft lessons 6–8](research/HATCH-CRAFT.md).

**Finish an arc, then invite the next.** Earlier powers and information should matter at the climax. Resolve the current book's central question, show the cost, and then seed continuation. A cliffhanger does not excuse abandoning the promised result. [Endings lesson](research/HATCH-CORE-AND-BING.md), [community discussion](research/ARTICLES-AND-REDDIT.md).

**Practice, completion, and revision remain essential.** Choose a sustainable schedule and a useful mix of outlining and discovery. Feedback should identify reader experiences before forcing someone else's fixes. The supplied Sanderson video is an introductory lecture, not his whole plotting or magic-system course. [Lecture notes](research/SANDERSON.md).

## Advice that needs a choice

| Tension in the sources | Our default |
| --- | --- |
| Many bonuses and messages versus notification fatigue | Keep rewards satisfying and legible. Show important changes; track the rest privately. Adjust to the intended audience and medium. |
| Overwhelming protagonists versus meaningful costs | Grant a real advantage, then choose limitations and challenges that still matter. Do not erase the advantage after every gain. |
| Cosmetic obstacles versus permanent losses | Decide the story's tone and failure stakes. Honor the costs that have been established. |
| Expert newcomer versus credible locals | Give the hero specific knowledge with a believable origin, and identify what locals know that the hero does not. |
| Male or solo protagonists versus other responsibilities | Character identity and party structure follow the story. A recommendation video on the same channel praises a mother protecting children. None of these is a compulsory protagonist type. |
| Fixed chapter maps versus flexible pacing | Use the functions to diagnose an outline. Combine, move, or expand them when that improves this story. |
| POV and tense preferences versus narrative needs | Pick a consistent voice and viewpoint plan that serves the chosen experience. Third-person past is an option. |
| Market-first advice versus personal creative aims | Define the audience and goal honestly. Commercial ambitions can guide choices without promising sales or invalidating personal writing. |

## What we do not turn into requirements

The Reddit post's ten steps are deliberately bad advice; the author confirms sarcasm. It is useful for recognizing failures such as meaningless numbers, prolonged tutorials, dependent companions, arbitrary advantages, and abandoned arcs. The discussion supplies individual counterpoints, not a representative audience survey.

Hatch's claims about forty-one shared ingredients, bestseller universality, dopamine timing, exact rankings, and income are not independently demonstrated by the supplied material. We preserve the suggestions and examples in attributed notes, without treating them as laws. His later, detailed System lesson offers more concrete rules-and-tracking guidance than a short promotional list can provide.

The launch videos add packaging, reserves, schedules, community fit, budgets, and measurement. Their Royal Road and Amazon algorithms, review mechanisms, costs, and eligibility claims need current official verification before operational use. They do not establish Pocket FM's rules. [Launch notes](research/HATCH-PROCESS-AND-LAUNCH.md), [follow-up cases](research/HATCH-LAUNCH-FOLLOWUP.md).

The owner's prose preference takes precedence over advice to shorten sentences: use simple explanations and natural rhythm. Our [twelve steps](PLAYBOOK.md), [templates](templates/), and [original examples](EXAMPLES.md) turn this research into work that can be reviewed and reused.
