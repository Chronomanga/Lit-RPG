# PlotWrite, Level Up, and LivingWriter

Research date: October 21, 2026. All three accessible main articles were read end to end. These are practitioner/editorial guides with product or submission promotion; their market claims were not independently verified.

## PlotWrite

[How to Write LitRPG: The Complete Guide for New Authors](https://www.plotwrite.ink/learn/litrpg-writing-guide), PlotWrite team. Published January 23, 2025; updated October 5, 2026. Coverage includes main sections, FAQ, quick answers, and the closing material.

Use a manageable attribute set, predictable derived values, and a chapter change record. Make skill acquisition answer a need, feel earned, and lead to a demonstration. Preserve meaningful losses and avoid redundant abilities; disclose rules through action and keep the story understandable without status panels. Its suggested stat, skill, chapter, and level intervals are heuristics without a demonstrated dataset here. A fixed arc count is optional. Retain controlled complexity and change-focused displays rather than using exact counts as quotas. Commercial-route advice needs separate current checks.

**Workflow use:** Steps 3, 5, 9, and 10: System scope, acquisition, selective display, continuity.

## Level Up Publishing

[How to write LitRPG](https://www.levelup.pub/how-to-write-litrpg). No displayed byline/date established. The article's first-person reference to writing Epic suggests Conor Kostick, whom the site's [About page](https://www.levelup.pub/about) identifies as its author/editor; this is an attribution inference. Complete introduction and five tips through closing invitation read.

Choose narration deliberately, maintain character-specific diction and viewpoint, and identify the source of System information. Reveal rules before important strategy so readers can judge the choice. Keep rewards from making prior build decisions meaningless. Distribute world knowledge through purposeful action or credible conversation. Give antagonists coherent reasons, preserve emotional development, and resolve a deliberate arc rather than extending indefinitely. The author labels the advice debatable. Preferences against first-person present and certain viewpoint descriptions are stylistic positions, not genre prohibitions. The strongest principle is making mechanics affect decisions instead of merely displaying them.

**Workflow use:** Steps 1–4, 6, and 10: voice, information, rules, motives, resolution.

## LivingWriter

[How to Write a LitRPG](https://livingwriter.com/blog/how-to-write-a-litrpg/), Alexandra Sise, May 5, 2022. Complete definition, five advice sections, and closing promotion read.

Reveal mechanics at need, maintain limits, justify unusual power, and give readers enough information to understand strategy. Differentiate the protagonist, companions, and antagonist; resolve a local book arc while connecting the series. Keep narrative voice consistent. Its otherworld-aware protagonist assumption is too narrow for native-world LitRPG, and claims about third-person emotional weakness or additional viewpoints are overgeneralizations. Read its exploit warning as a concern about arbitrary exceptions, not a ban on established clever loopholes. The description of a new genre is 2022 framing, and product recommendations are promotion.

**Workflow use:** Steps 2, 3, 6, 9, and 10: people, rules, closure, disclosure, consistency.

## Our original implementation

The common useful questions become a premise brief, narration choice, System specification, progression record, causal chapter card, and final revision. The playbook adds an explicit living-world pass, natural-flow style, original calculation examples, and later audio adaptation. Those worksheets are our designs, not source templates or measured guarantees.
