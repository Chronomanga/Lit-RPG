# Later Pocket FM adaptation

This repository starts with the story. Use this stage when the owner requests Pocket FM work. A novel chapter and an audio episode are different units; do not convert by a fixed ratio or pad the story to reach a number.

## Adapt the manuscript

1. Choose episode boundaries around coherent scenes and meaningful changes. If a break weakens cause and effect, move it.
2. Give every episode a clear, distinct title. Keep its number, title, and actual platform sequence mapped together.
3. Preserve a natural spoken flow. Common words and clear explanations do not require clipped sentences.
4. Translate visual System displays into clear spoken information. Read only values needed to understand the choice or gain. A visual table may need a short spoken summary rather than every cell.
5. Introduce speakers and maintain point of view so a listener can follow without seeing the page.
6. Use earned continuation hooks, with real results along the way. Do not turn every ending into an interrupted action.
7. Keep a mapping from adapted text to the underlying chapter and canonical state. An edit in one place may require another version of the audio or later episodes.

## Working length defaults

The owner's existing Story Playbook uses **at least 1,200 story-text characters and no more than 1,500 words per final episode**. This is an inherited workflow default, not a verified claim about Pocket FM's current official minimum or maximum. Do not confuse 1,200 characters with 1,200 words. The current owner request and verified destination requirements determine any adjustment.

Count the actual prose after final polish, with the counting method stated. Do not pad a scene with routine explanations to reach a target. Recheck official or account-specific requirements at the point of submission.

## Audio review, if audio is produced

Preserve a versioned script and audio pairing. Verify the final encoded export, not only an intermediate preview. A full normal-speed listen checks:

- Added, repeated, skipped, broken, or clipped words.
- Pronunciation, names, numbers, and System wording.
- Awkward mid-phrase gaps, broken joins, unintended stop-start delivery, and excessive sentence gaps.
- Loudness changes and music or effects masking speech.
- Character and emotional delivery, natural breaths, and intentional dramatic silence.

Record defects with timestamps and surrounding words. Repair in a new version, replay both joins, and listen through the complete final export. Transcription, waveform, silence detection, file decoding, and duration are supporting checks; they do not replace listening.

If full listening was not possible, state that it remains pending. Do not ask the owner to reconfirm a line already approved without a new reason, and do not describe a technical check as a human hearing judgment.

## Publication and state

Respect the owner's explicit current authorization, including an instruction to publish each finished episode. Do not invent authorization from a destination mentioned only as a future plan. Verify the actual book, account, sequence, and text before any write.

Record distinct states: local manuscript, saved creator draft, submitted, creator-side published, publicly available to listeners, and audio fully listened to. Read back the exact final text and relevant platform state after an authorized write. A creator badge alone does not establish public listener availability.

Royal Road launch recommendations in the research are not Pocket FM requirements. Verify current official policies before using reviews, promotions, scheduling, or monetization tactics. The current research does not establish those policies.
